### CHEM-000542: Stable direct CO₂-to-methanol electrosynthesis
- **Subfield:** Carbon-dioxide electrochemistry
- **Open:** Combine durable methanol-selective molecular catalysis, low full-cell energy use and useful product concentration without catalyst loss or restructuring.
- **Status:** Song et al. reached 132 mA cm⁻² methanol partial current and 62% selectivity near pH 1 in 2025. Long-duration performance including recovery remains open.
- **Type:** engineering
- **Why:** It could supply a major liquid feedstock directly from CO₂ and electricity.
- **Key works:** Y. Song et al. (2025), *Efficient CO2-to-methanol electrocatalysis in acidic media via microenvironment-tuned cobalt phthalocyanine*, [DOI:10.1038/s41565-025-02059-z](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41565-025-02059-z).
